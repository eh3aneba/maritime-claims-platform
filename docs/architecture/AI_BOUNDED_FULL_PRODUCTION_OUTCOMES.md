# Sprint 11S — Bounded 100% Outcome and Enterprise Production Readiness

Sprint 11S is a recommendation-only measurement plane over one completed Sprint 11R bounded 100% Production AI cohort. It cannot widen runtime scope.

## Immutable anchor
An assessment may start only when the Sprint 11R authorization is completed, still linked to the same positive Sprint 11Q recommendation, has immutable decision/completion hashes, a fresh passing final monitor, complete non-safety recovery and zero Privacy/Security/Cross-tenant incident history.

The assessment freezes the Sprint 11R decision/completion hashes, Sprint 11Q assessment/decision hashes, inherited Sprint 11P decision/completion hashes, exact model/prompt/schema bundle, document allowlist, rollout and caps.

## Source-ledger recomputation
Sprint 11S re-reads actual Sprint 11R run, monitor and incident rows. It recomputes human-review coverage, different-human review, Reject/Edit rates, unsupported outputs, grounding, latency, provider cost, trend regression, cap use, incident history and rollback recovery. Copied aggregate metrics are not sufficient.

## Business evidence
At least 15 fresh content-free baseline-versus-assisted workflows are required. Median Time-to-First-Assessment improvement must be at least 35%, triage/chronology improvement at least 45% and handler-effort improvement at least 30%, with no aggregate increase in rework, escalation or correction and 100% human ownership of authoritative claim decisions.

## Enterprise readiness evidence
Ten distinct content-free control categories must each be present and passing:
- kill switch / rollback;
- monitor freshness and alerting;
- audit-log and hash-chain traceability;
- tenant isolation;
- privacy / data protection;
- availability / recovery;
- model-prompt-schema change control;
- unit economics;
- human escalation ownership;
- incident response and executive production ownership.

## Independent review
Fourteen distinct non-requesting reviewers are required: Security, Privacy, Product, Operations, Risk, Claims Governance, AI Quality, Legal/Data Governance, Business Owner, Platform Reliability, Independent Production Assurance, Data Protection, Executive Production Sponsor and Enterprise Architecture / Operational Resilience. Final Admin must be different from the requester and all reviewers.

## Hard boundary
Even `recommend_separate_production_wide_authorization_review` is only a recommendation. Sprint 11S never authorizes unbounded Production-wide AI, Restricted documents, new document classes, autonomous claim decisions, automatic authoritative facts or removal of different-human review.
