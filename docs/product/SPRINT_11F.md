# Sprint 11F — Measured Limited-Production Outcome

## Goal

Measure a completed Sprint 11E limited-production cohort and record a separate stop, extend or consider-graduation recommendation without widening rollout automatically.

## Delivered controls

- append-only tenant-scoped assessments anchored to the exact completed 11E authorization;
- exact decision-hash/model/prompt/schema/rollout pinning;
- one content-free observation for every immutable human-reviewed provider run;
- deterministic cohort and CE Report / Engine Log scorecards;
- fixed Reject/Edit, usefulness, unsupported-output, source-grounding, effort, latency and cost thresholds;
- first-half vs second-half quality, latency and cost regression checks;
- zero-tolerance graduation boundary for Privacy/Security/Cross-tenant incidents;
- zero unresolved High/Critical incidents;
- four distinct non-requesting Product/Quality/Risk/Operations reviewers;
- Admin-only stop, extend or recommend-graduation outcome;
- immutable SHA-256 assessment and decision snapshots;
- API, web operator page, migration, tests, audit events, architecture documentation and ADR.

## Non-goals

Sprint 11F does not:

- increase the recorded 1–10% rollout;
- authorize Production-wide AI;
- authorize Restricted documents or new document classes;
- provision or mutate provider infrastructure;
- bypass human review;
- make liability, coverage, reserve, settlement or payment decisions;
- automatically update authoritative claim facts.

## Exit

A positive result means only: design a separately authorized graduation/scale-up stage.
