# Controlled Paid Pilot Offer v1.0

## Purpose
Validate whether MCRI reduces H&M machinery claims handling effort and improves evidence control in the customer’s real workflow. This is a controlled product pilot, not production outsourcing or automated claim decision-making.

## Suggested duration
30 days.

## Suggested scope
- Up to 5–10 selected H&M machinery claims.
- One primary claims team / business unit.
- Approved anonymized, synthetic, or customer-authorized evidence only.
- Document intake, AI-assisted structured extraction, human review, missing documents, chronology/conflicts, technical review, financial review, tasks and initial assessment.

## MCRI deliverables
- Pilot environment and onboarding.
- Configuration of initial users and access.
- Controlled case setup.
- Pilot instrumentation and scorecard.
- Weekly founder review of false positives / workflow friction.
- End-of-pilot value and commercial review.

## Customer responsibilities
- Nominate a claims champion and commercial/buyer contact.
- Provide approved pilot files and confirm data-use permissions.
- Establish a manual baseline for selected tasks where possible.
- Participate in structured pilot sessions and feedback.
- Identify security/procurement requirements early.

## Success criteria
Initial targets to test, not contractual warranties:
- ≥30% reduction in time to initial assessment versus an agreed baseline.
- ≥80% AI suggestion acceptance on reviewed extraction candidates.
- ≥90% precision for human-validated missing-document suggestions.
- User rating ≥8/10.
- No unresolved critical product-safety issue.
- A documented business case / next step at pilot completion.

## Explicit exclusions
- Automated coverage determination.
- Automated liability determination.
- Confirmed root-cause determination by AI.
- Automated reserve prediction.
- Automated settlement/payment.
- Production SLA unless separately agreed.
- Use of customer data for cross-customer model training without explicit agreement.

## Pricing test anchors
For discovery/testing only; not a published price list:
- Controlled paid pilot: USD 5k / 10k / 20k anchors depending on scope.
- Annual platform discussion: USD 20k / 40k / 75k anchors after evidence of value.

Always ask for unprompted willingness-to-pay before presenting anchors.
