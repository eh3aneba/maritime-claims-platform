## Account notes and evidence

### 1. Harren Ship Management / Harren Group

**Why it fits:** Harren publishes Insurance & Claims as part of ship management and lists dedicated Directors Insurance & Claims. Its broader ship-management offering includes maintenance/engineering and asset-management functions, which should create the same cross-document machinery-claim workflow MCRI targets.

**Discovery hypothesis:** claims staff spend significant time assembling technical evidence, repair quotations, chronology and correspondence across internal technical teams and external insurers/adjusters.

**Best first ask:** 30-minute workflow discovery around one anonymised machinery casualty; do not lead with a paid pilot.

**Public contacts:** Falk Fischer and Lars Kempers — Director Insurance & Claims; `insurance@harren-group.com`.

Sources:
- https://www.harren-group.com/ship-management/team
- https://www.harren-group.com/ship-management

### 2. Alandia

**Why it fits:** Alandia is a specialist marine insurer offering Hull & Machinery and publishes a dedicated H&M claims team. Its claims process explicitly follows the case from notification through repair strategy, matching MCRI's current workflow.

**Discovery hypothesis:** source-linked document review, chronology and missing-document intelligence could reduce first-review and assessment workload without asking Alandia to delegate coverage judgment to AI.

**Best first ask:** claims-workflow discovery with H&M team; position MCRI as evidence/claims productivity, not automated coverage.

**Public contact:** Johan Henriksson — Head of Marine Claims; `johan.henriksson@alandia.com`; general `marineclaims@alandia.com`.

Sources:
- https://alandia.com/commercial-marine/claims-service/claims-contact/
- https://alandia.com/commercial-marine/claims-service/claims-handling-process/
- https://alandia.com/commercial-marine/insurance-products/hull-machinery/

### 3. Thomas Schulte Ship Management

**Why it fits:** TSM publicly lists insurance portfolio management, claims handling/processing, case strategy, communication with adjusters and technical management. This is a direct functional match for MCRI.

**Discovery hypothesis:** MCRI can bridge technical department evidence and insurance/claims administration into one auditable claim workspace.

**Best first ask:** discovery with Technical Director or insurance-responsible colleague; ask to map the current machinery-claim information flow.

**Public contacts:** Alexander Schulte — CEO; Jan Paninka — Technical Director; `fleet-de@tsm-shipmanagement.com`.

Source:
- https://www.tsm-shipmanagement.com/

### 4. Tschudi Ship Management

**Why it fits:** Tschudi explicitly offers insurance management and claims handling alongside technical management, vessel repairs and consultancy.

**Discovery hypothesis:** smaller management organisation could validate whether MCRI is useful without the procurement burden of a global manager.

**Best first ask:** COO/Fleet Manager discovery session using synthetic MT ORION workflow.

**Public contacts:** Martin Kruus — COO; Glib Lyekontsev — Fleet Manager; `info@tschudishipmanagement.com`.

Sources:

### 5. Alassia NewShips Management

**Why it fits:** Alassia publishes technical management and Insurance & Claims Management for its dry-bulk fleet. It also publicly reported joining an AI initiative with Complexio, a useful (but not conclusive) signal of openness to digital workflows.

**Discovery hypothesis:** machinery evidence and repair-cost workflow should be accessible internally because technical, operations and insurance/claims functions sit in the same manager.

**Best first ask:** workflow discovery through Technical Department; avoid pitching AI novelty and focus on claim-handling cycle time and evidence control.

**Public contacts:** `technical@alassia.eu`, `admin@alassia.eu`.

Sources:

- https://www.alassia.eu/

## Recommended founder sequence

1. **Harren** — direct insurance/claims leadership and right-sized ship-management context.
2. **Alandia** — strongest insurer workflow fit and direct H&M claims contact.
3. **Thomas Schulte** — very direct claims/technical functional match in Germany.
4. **Tschudi** — likely lower pilot friction and public operational contacts.
5. **Alassia** — technically aligned and public innovation signal.
6. British Marine if a warm path exists.
7. Norwegian Hull Club after one smaller pilot result is available.
8. BSM/OSM Thome after security/deployment proof is stronger.

## Information that still must be learned in discovery

Do **not** treat the following as known from public research:

- actual annual H&M machinery-claim count;
- exact claims team headcount;
- current claims system/vendor;
- internal document-processing time;
- real buyer/budget owner;
- willingness to share anonymised claim files;
- information-security approval path;
- willingness to pay;
- current AI policy.

These fields stay `unknown` until a real conversation supplies evidence.
