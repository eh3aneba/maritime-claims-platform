# First Design Partner Cohort v1.0

## Objective
Build a founder-led cohort of 3–5 qualified design partners and convert at least one into a paid controlled pilot. Qualification precedes demo; a logo is not valuable if the account lacks H&M machinery volume, accessible users, usable data, or a buying path.

## Beachhead order
1. **Medium marine insurer / H&M claims team** — highest product-workflow match.
2. **Ship manager with meaningful machinery casualty volume** — strong operational pain and data access; buyer may differ from claims user.
3. **Average adjuster / marine claims specialist** — excellent expert validator and workflow partner; commercial ACV may be lower.
4. **P&I correspondent** — useful secondary design partner, but the current MVP is H&M-heavy rather than P&I-first.
5. Large P&I Club / top-tier enterprise — defer until private deployment, browser gates, security package, and repeatable pilot evidence are stronger.

## Cohort composition target
- 2 marine insurers / H&M claims teams
- 2 ship managers / technical managers with claims responsibility
- 1 marine claims / adjusting specialist

This is a sampling strategy, not a claim that these segments will ultimately be the best market.

## Qualification score (100)
- Pain intensity: 25
- Machinery claim volume: 20
- Buyer access: 20
- Data availability: 15
- Security fit: 10
- Pilot willingness: 10

Each component is scored 0–5.

### Bands
- A: 75–100 — active founder priority
- B: 60–74 — pursue after A-tier / resolve gaps
- C: 40–59 — nurture only
- D: <40 — do not spend active founder time

A high score ranks attention; it does **not** predict a purchase.

## Minimum qualification before controlled pilot
A partner should normally have all of:
- at least one relevant H&M machinery claim available for controlled testing;
- a named claims/technical user;
- a reachable buyer or budget owner hypothesis;
- permission to use anonymized/synthetic or approved claim files;
- willingness to attend a measured pilot session;
- no unresolved security requirement that makes the current MVP impossible to test.

## Funnel target
Target account list → 8–12 qualified prospects → 5 discovery conversations → 3 controlled design-partner pilots → 1 paid pilot.

The numbers are operating targets, not forecast conversion rates.
