# Cohort Operating Cadence

## Weekly founder review
Review the outreach dashboard once per week:
- A-tier accounts with no next step;
- discovery meetings scheduled/completed;
- accounts stalled >14 days;
- pilot-qualified accounts;
- offers shared / accepted;
- top objections by category;
- source of warm introductions;
- cohort goal: 3 qualified partners / 1 paid pilot.

## Stage definitions
- Prospect: identified, not contacted.
- Contacted: at least one real touch sent.
- Discovery: discovery meeting completed or scheduled with substantive engagement.
- Demo: workflow demo performed.
- Pilot Qualified: user/data/buyer path sufficient for a controlled pilot.
- Pilot Proposed: explicit pilot scope proposed.
- Pilot Active: controlled unpaid or paid validation underway.
- Paid Pilot: customer has accepted a paid pilot.
- Customer: converted to continuing commercial relationship.
- No Fit: explicitly disqualified.

Do not advance stages solely because an email was opened or someone said the product was interesting.
