## What remains unknown

Public research does not establish:

- actual H&M machinery claim volume;
- claims team headcount;
- current claims software stack;
- buyer/budget ownership;
- pilot willingness;
- internal AI policy;
- data-sharing/security path;
- willingness to pay.

These remain discovery questions and must not be filled by inference.

## Outreach launch condition

The founder can begin outreach after choosing the preferred sequence and channel. The recommended initial objective is a 30-minute workflow-discovery conversation using synthetic MT ORION data, not a product sale.

## Success metric for this phase

Top-of-funnel operating target:

- 8–12 researched accounts
- 5 founder-priority accounts
- 3 qualified design partners
- at least 1 paid pilot

Only the first two are complete in Sprint 6D. Qualification and paid-pilot outcomes require real external conversations.
