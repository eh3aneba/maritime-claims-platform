## Decision safeguards
- Commercial validation cannot change claim evidence, coverage, causation, reserve, recoverability, or settlement.
- Missing ROI assumptions remain `Not measured`.
- STOP is a session-level recommendation and requires explicit no-interest with no WTP/next-step signal.
- GO requires measured product evidence plus buyer, champion, WTP, and next-step signals.
- Early ROI monetizes time/labor capacity only; avoided leakage or settlement savings are excluded until empirically demonstrated.
