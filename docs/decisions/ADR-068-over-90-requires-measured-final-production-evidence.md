# ADR-068 — Rollout Above 90% Requires Measured Final-Production Evidence

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
Sprint 11N allows a separately authorized, bounded 76–90% Production AI cohort. Completion of that cohort proves only that its explicit envelope completed under its controls. It does not prove that the remaining >90% population, or Production-wide operation, is safe or economically justified.

## Decision
Before any authorization above 90% can be designed, the platform must complete Sprint 11O: a source-ledger-based outcome assessment of one completed Sprint 11N cohort. The gate requires at least 120 different-human-reviewed provider runs, complete content-free outcome observations, higher-coverage business-value revalidation, fresh monitoring, full recovery evidence, zero safety-boundary incident history and ten independent reviews plus a separate Admin decision.

A positive Sprint 11O result is recommendation-only. It cannot activate a 91–100% cohort, Production-wide AI, new document classes, Restricted documents, autonomous claim decisions or automatic authoritative claim facts.

## Consequences
The rollout path remains intentionally discontinuous. A completed 76–90% cohort cannot auto-widen. Any future >90% stage must be separately specified, approved, expiring, reversible and human-governed, and must anchor to the immutable Sprint 11O assessment and decision hashes.
