# ADR-028 — Commercial validation is evidence-driven and separate from claim decisions

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
Design-partner commercial validation will be stored per pilot session and will explicitly capture buyer/champion roles, buying stage, budget path, WTP signals, deployment/security requirements, blockers, and next step.

ROI calculations will use only recorded pilot timing and explicit commercial assumptions. Missing assumptions remain unmeasured. Early ROI will not monetize claim leakage or settlement/recovery improvements without empirical evidence.

GO/PIVOT/STOP is a product-validation recommendation. It must never alter claim evidence, coverage, causation, reserve, cost recoverability, or settlement.

## Rationale
Positive user feedback is not sufficient proof of a viable buying process. Product telemetry and commercial evidence need to be combined without conflating them. Conservative ROI and explicit unknowns reduce the risk of manufacturing a business case from assumptions.
