# ADR-029 — Design-partner accounts are qualified before demo

## Decision
Founder time is ranked using an explicit H&M-relevant qualification score before a controlled demo or pilot is prioritized.

## Rationale
Logo prestige and generic interest do not validate an H&M claims product. Early founder time should favor accounts with real machinery claim pain, usable evidence, accessible users/buyers and practical security conditions.

## Consequences
- Score ranks outreach priority but does not predict purchasing.
- Large enterprises may intentionally rank below smaller accessible design partners.
- Stage advancement requires evidence of engagement, not vanity activity.
